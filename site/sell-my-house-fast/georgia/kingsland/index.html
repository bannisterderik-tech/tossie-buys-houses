What selling in Kingsland actually involves

Because Georgia is an attorney closing state, the closing calendar in Camden County is set by the attorney's availability more than by us. Kingsland is known for Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay commuter market, and that shapes the kind of houses we see here. Humidity does the damage here. Left unconditioned, a closed-up Kingsland house grows mold in weeks.

Deferred maintenance compounds in this climate. What a Kingsland owner puts off in spring is a bigger number by the following spring.

Selling to us skips the parts that cost you time and money: no listing, no showings, no appraisal, no inspection contingency, and no repair negotiation after an inspector writes his report. We are paying cash, so there is no lender deciding whether your house qualifies.

Georgia foreclosure, in one paragraph: Almost every Georgia foreclosure is non-judicial. No judge signs off, and the sale happens on the courthouse steps on the first Tuesday of the month. There is no right of redemption after a non-judicial foreclosure sale in Georgia. The 12-month redemption people hear about applies only to judicial foreclosures, which are rare here.
